# 📊 Employee Attrition Analysis using Excel

## 🎯 Problem Statement & Objective
Employee turnover is one of the most critical operational and financial drains on an organization. The objective of this project is to analyze historical employee data in Excel to answer the core business question: 
> *"How can leadership proactively reduce employee attrition, identify high-risk segments, and improve long-term retention?"*

This project empowers HR professionals and management with data-driven insights to shift from reactive firefighting to targeted retention strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Methodology
* **Tool:** Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, Calculated Fields, Slicers, Charts)
* **Dataset Size:** 2,925 employee records across 37+ attributes (Demographics, Compensation, Job Roles, Performance Ratings).
* **Data Hygiene:** Handled missing values, standardized categorical headers, synchronized attrition flags, and engineered tenure/overtime helper columns.

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Findings

1. **Overall Attrition Rate:** 
   * The organization maintains a baseline turnover rate of **16.17%** (473 departures out of 2,925 total employees).
   
2. **High-Risk Job Roles & Departments:**
   * **Sales Representatives** experience an alarming attrition rate of **40.48%**, followed by **Laboratory Technicians (~24%)** and **Human Resources (~23%)**.
   * The **Sales Department** overall registers the highest departmental turnover at **20.72%**.

3. **The Burnout Factor (Overtime):**
   * Employees working **Over Time (Yes)** face a dramatic **30.59% attrition rate** compared to just **10.49%** for standard working hours, pointing directly to workplace burnout as a primary driver.

4. **Age & Early-Career Mobility:**
   * Younger employees show high turnover: staff **Under 25 years old** record a **39.18%** attrition rate, and the **25–34 age bracket** stands at **20.31%**.

---

## 💡 Actionable HR Recommendations
* **Audit & Manage Overtime:** Restructure workloads in high-pressure teams and introduce compensatory policies to mitigate burnout.
* **Targeted Onboarding for Entry-Level Roles:** Implement structured 30-60-90 day mentorship programs for Sales Representatives and employees under 25.
* **Competitive Compensation & Career Pathing:** Review compensation bands for lower-income tiers and establish clear internal promotion pathways.
